EXPERIENCED FOUNDERS & OPERATORS Commercial EIRs • Deal Origination • Founding Equity

Are You an Experienced Founder or Commercial Operator?

Most pre-seed software fails not from weak engineering, but from a lack of senior distribution instincts. If you have founded a company, navigated procurement, or closed high-ticket B2B deals before, partner with Mamba MVP. We provide the full-stack engineering rails, pre-vetted AI software products, and pre-seed capital—you drive customer discovery, unlock enterprise accounts, and capture direct deal-by-deal cash origination and founding equity upside.

Commercial EIR & Venture Builder

Partner with early-stage technical founders to architect enterprise go-to-market wedges, customer discovery, and pilot agreements. Steer commercial execution without having to manage engineering sprints from scratch.

  • Shape commercial product-market fit and enterprise pricing
  • Direct strategic collaboration with Venture Partner James Sun
  • Full engineering and rapid prototyping backed by studio developers

Deal Origination & Revenue Share

Monetize your industry relationships and commercial acumen. Earn 15% to 25% direct cash origination on closed enterprise AI software contracts ($20k to $150k+ ACV), wired upon customer milestone settlement.

  • 15% to 25% direct cash origination on closed annual enterprise ACV
  • Immediate, transparent milestone payout rails via corporate accounts
  • Carry allocations and advisory equity in breakout studio companies

Commercial Co-Founder Runway

Test-drive traction and team chemistry during our studio sprints before taking the full-time leap. Step in as Founding Commercial Co-Founder, Chief Commercial Officer (CCO), or CEO of breakout spin-outs.

  • Substantial founding equity grants (5%–20%) with institutional vesting
  • Exclusive co-investment allocation on studio terms alongside GPs
  • Direct pathway to Series Seed institutional syndicates at Demo Day
Are you an experienced founder, commercial executive, or B2B operator? Explore Commercial EIR, Growth Partner, and Co-Founder engagements with our studio team.
