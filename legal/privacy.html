Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Mamba Venture Program collects, why, who sees it and how long we keep it. It is written to meet Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and the guidance of the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

1. Who we are

The Mamba Venture Program ("MVP", "we", "us") is owned and operated by Mamba Partners Pte. Ltd. (UEN [Mamba Partners UEN]), [Mamba Partners registered address]. Mamba Partners Pte. Ltd. is the organisation responsible for the personal data described here.

Eve Count (UEN 53438315K), trading as Cybrdeck is the programme's technology and engineering partner. It runs this website, the application form and the systems behind them on Mamba Partners' behalf, as a data intermediary under the PDPA. SG Innovation is the programme's ecosystem partner.

Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at james@mambapartners.com.

2. What this policy covers

This website (mambaventureprogram.web.app and any domain that points to it), the application form, emails we send you about your application, and the programme itself if you join a cohort. Events booked through Luma are also covered by Luma's own privacy policy.

3. What we collect

When you apply

  • Contact details: your name, mobile number and email address.
  • Background: your current situation, company or employer, role, years of experience, and links you choose to share (LinkedIn, portfolio, GitHub).
  • Your venture: your idea or venture, the problem, evidence, competitors, stage, customers, business model, team and what you have built.
  • Eligibility and grant fit: residency status, whether you are a first-time founder, co-founders, incorporation, local equity, proprietary technology, capital you could match, overseas plans and prior public funding. We ask these because Singapore grant schemes depend on them.
  • Commitment and circumstances: your availability, how long you could cover living costs, who depends on your income and whether your employer knows you are applying. These help us judge fit and timing; you can give a general answer.
  • Third parties you name: if you name someone we may contact to verify a claim or a milestone, we collect their name and contact details. Please tell them before you do.

We do not ask for your NRIC, FIN or passport number. Please do not include them, or any health, financial account or other sensitive information, in free-text answers.

When you browse

Our hosting provider records standard technical data (IP address, browser, pages requested and time) to deliver and secure the site. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

When you contact us or RSVP

The details you give us in emails. Event RSVPs are collected by Luma, which shares the guest list with us.

4. How we use it

We collect and use your personal data with your consent, given when you submit the application, and only for these purposes:

  • to assess your application, shortlist and interview applicants, and decide on places in a cohort;
  • to send you a receipt, and to contact you about your application, interviews and the cohort;
  • to run the programme if you join, including matching you with mentors and partners and pointing you to grant schemes;
  • to verify claims you make, only with people you have named for that purpose;
  • to review and improve the programme using aggregate figures (for example how many applied and from which industries), in which no individual is identified;
  • to keep the site and our systems secure and to meet our legal obligations.

We will not use your data for any other purpose without telling you and, where the PDPA requires it, asking for your consent. We do not sell personal data, and we do not send marketing messages to Singapore telephone numbers without your clear consent, in line with the Do Not Call provisions of the PDPA.

5. AI-assisted review

To help assessors read applications consistently, each application is scored against our published criteria by a rules-based system, and a large language model (Qwen, run on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio in Singapore) adds a review note and draft interview questions. Before any text is sent to the model, we remove your name, contact details, employer, company name, links and identifiers such as phone numbers and NRIC-style numbers.

The model can move a score only within a small, fixed range, and it does not make decisions. Every decision about your application is made by people. Your application is not used to train any AI model. You can ask us how your application was assessed (see section 10).

6. Who sees it

  • Assessors: the Mamba Partners programme lead and the Cybrdeck founders who review applications. They receive your application as a document by email and in a private, access-controlled shared drive.
  • Programme partners: if you join a cohort, mentors and partners who work with you see what they need to help you. We tell you before introducing you to an investor or corporate partner.
  • Our technology partner, Eve Count (UEN 53438315K), trading as Cybrdeck, which operates the site and application systems for us.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract and only on our instructions: Google (Firebase hosting, database and cloud functions, Google Workspace for email and drive) and Alibaba Cloud (the AI review in section 5).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

7. Where it is stored

Your application is stored in Google Cloud in Singapore (asia-southeast1), and the AI review runs in Alibaba Cloud's Singapore region. Emails and the shared drive run on Google Workspace, which may store data in other countries. Where data leaves Singapore, we rely on our providers' contractual commitments to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA, as required by section 26 of the PDPA and the Personal Data Protection Regulations 2021.

8. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as it serves the purposes above or the law requires. Our retention schedule sets out the periods. In short: applications that do not lead to a place are deleted 12 months after the cohort decision, so we can consider you for the next intake. If you accept a place, the Founder Data Protection Policy in your offer letter applies from then on, and participant records may be kept for up to 10 years for legal, regulatory, record-keeping and continuing commercial purposes.

9. How we protect it

We make reasonable security arrangements, including encryption in transit and at rest, a database that the public can write to but not read, access limited to named assessors, and secrets kept in a managed secret store. If a data breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the PDPC as required by Part 6A of the PDPA. Our data protection policy has more detail.

10. Your choices and rights

Under the PDPA you can:

  • Access: ask what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year;
  • Correct: ask us to correct an error or omission;
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw your consent at any time. We will tell you the likely consequences (for example, we cannot continue to consider your application), stop using your data and delete it unless the law requires us to keep it.

Email james@mambapartners.com from the address you applied with. We will reply within 30 days, or tell you if we need longer. We may need to confirm your identity first. We do not charge for reasonable requests.

11. Cookies and your device

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The application form saves your answers in your browser's local storage so you can stop and come back; this stays on your device, is cleared when you submit, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

12. Other websites

Links to Luma, LinkedIn and other sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

13. Age

The programme is for adults. Please do not apply if you are under 18.

14. Changes

We will post any change here with a new date. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you by email.

15. Contact and complaints

Write to our Data Protection Officer at james@mambapartners.com, or by post to Mamba Partners Pte. Ltd., [Mamba Partners registered address]. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission at pdpc.gov.sg.
