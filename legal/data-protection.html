Data protection policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

How the Mamba Venture Program meets its obligations under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) in practice: what we hold, where it lives, how long we keep it, how it is protected, and what happens if something goes wrong. Our privacy policy is the notice to you; this is how we keep it.

1. Purpose

This policy applies to Mamba JJ Partners Pte. Ltd., Eve Count (UEN 53438315K), trading as Cybrdeck as our data intermediary, the people who assess applications, and program partners who receive personal data from us. It covers all personal data collected through the site, the application form, email and the program.

2. Roles

  • Organisation responsible: Mamba JJ Partners Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202409076E), owner and operator of the program.
  • Data intermediary: Eve Count (UEN 53438315K), trading as Cybrdeck, which operates the website, application form, database and review pipeline on Mamba Partners' instructions.
  • Data Protection Officer: james@mambapartners.com. The DPO handles requests, keeps this policy current, approves new uses of data and leads breach response.
  • Assessors (Cybrdeck founders and the Mamba Partners program lead) may read applications for assessment and must not copy them outside the systems listed in section 4.
  • Data intermediaries (section 10) process data only on our instructions.

3. Our commitments

We follow the PDPA obligations: consent, purpose limitation, notification, access and correction, accuracy, protection, retention limitation, transfer limitation, accountability and data breach notification. In practice:

  • we collect only what the application needs, and say why for the sensitive questions;
  • we do not collect NRIC, FIN or passport numbers;
  • new uses of existing data need the DPO's approval and, where required, fresh consent;
  • personal data is never sold or used for advertising.

6. Security measures

  • All traffic uses HTTPS; providers encrypt data at rest.
  • Database rules let the public form create an application but never read, change or delete one.
  • Application data is validated on the server; malformed submissions are rejected.
  • API keys and email credentials are kept in Google Secret Manager, never in code.
  • The review drive is a restricted shared drive; access is granted by name and removed when someone leaves the program team.
  • Accounts with access use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication.
  • Assessors must not download applications to personal devices or forward them outside the program team.

7. AI and automated scoring

Following the PDPC's Advisory Guidelines on the Use of Personal Data in AI Recommendation and Decision Systems:

  • scores come from published, rules-based criteria; the AI review can adjust them only within a fixed range;
  • names, contact details, employer, company, links and ID-like numbers are removed before text reaches the model;
  • the model provider does not use the text to train models;
  • a person makes every decision, and applicants can ask how their application was assessed.

8. Data breaches

  1. Contain: anyone who suspects a breach tells the DPO immediately; access is revoked and keys rotated as needed.
  2. Assess: the DPO assesses within 30 days whether the breach is notifiable, meaning it is likely to cause significant harm or affects 500 or more people.
  3. Notify: if notifiable, we notify the PDPC within 3 calendar days of that assessment, and affected individuals as soon as practicable where there is likely significant harm.
  4. Learn: we record what happened and what we changed.

9. Access, correction and withdrawal

Requests go to james@mambapartners.com. We confirm identity by replying to the email address on the application, respond within 30 days (or tell you when we will), and keep a record of each request and its outcome. Withdrawal of consent stops processing and starts deletion under section 5.
