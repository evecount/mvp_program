Mentor, invest or partner

Every MVP cohort is built with people who have done it before. If you mentor founders, invest early, build ventures or run an ecosystem, there is a place for you on the panel.

Who we work with

Mentors and domain advisorsEngineering leads, former founders and commercial experts who give cohort founders one-to-one time on the problem they are stuck on.
Angel investorsMeet founders at demo and pitch sessions and co-invest in cohort graduates on standard early-stage terms.
VC fundsEarly sight of ventures with real customer evidence, and a say in what investor-ready looks like for each cohort.
Venture builders and studiosCo-build, share deal flow, or run a track with us.
Incubators and ecosystem partnersHost sessions, refer founders, and connect cohorts to your programs and spaces.
Venture and commercial partnersBring distribution, go-to-market and sales experience to ventures as they find their first customers.
Corporate and service partnersPilot with cohort startups, or offer legal, banking, cloud and compliance support.
